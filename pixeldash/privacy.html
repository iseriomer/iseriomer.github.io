02 Information We Collect

We believe in privacy-by-design. We do not require you to provide government-issued identification, full legal name, physical street address, or phone numbers to play our Games. The data collected falls into the following categories:

A. Information Automatically Collected by Devices & SDKs

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, system language, screen resolution, and available memory for performance optimization.
  • Network & Diagnostics: Coarse geolocation (country or city level derived from IP address; we do not access precise GPS location), network connectivity type (Wi-Fi/Cellular), crash reports, and game session logs.
  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple ID for Advertisers (IDFA), subject to your explicit device-level and in-app consent preferences.

B. Information Generated During Gameplay

  • Player Profiles & Nicknames: Self-selected in-game display names (pseudonyms) created by you to identify yourself in multiplayer lobbies and leaderboards.
  • Game Progress & Telemetry: High scores, wave progression, unlocked characters, virtual currency balances (gold, gems), chest unlock timestamps, and matchmaking ELO ratings.
  • Multiplayer Match Data: Temporary game room codes, real-time swipe/movement coordinates transmitted between peers, and match outcome timestamps.

C. In-App Purchase & Financial Information

All financial transactions (such as purchasing "No Ads" or "Starter Pack") are processed exclusively and securely by official store platforms (Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchases). We do never collect, receive, store, or process your credit card numbers, bank credentials, or billing addresses.

05 Advertising & Google AdMob

Our free-to-play Games are monetized partly through third-party advertising provided by Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's Mobile Advertising ID to serve ads.

Ad Format Transparency:

Our Games utilize strictly capped ad placements, such as voluntary Rewarded Video Ads (which players choose to watch for in-game perks) and frequency-capped Interstitial Ads. We implement strict frequency capping (minimum cooldown timers and death limits) to ensure advertisements never compromise user experience.

You can opt out of interest-based personalized advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Toggle off allow apps to ask to track.
  • In-Game: You can purchase the "Remove Ads" package to permanently eliminate all non-rewarded interstitial ads.
