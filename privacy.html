Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Pixel Dash: Infinite Maze ("the App"), developed by Ömer Faruk İşeri. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to provide personally identifiable information (such as real name, email address, or phone number) to play. We may process:

  • Game Progress & Nickname: In-game player nicknames chosen by you, scores, and currency stored locally on your device and synchronized via Firebase for online multiplayer and leaderboards.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, and general gameplay metrics for stability and performance.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID used by Google AdMob to deliver advertisements in compliance with applicable laws.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App integrates trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob: Used for serving rewarded, interstitial, and banner ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers and ad interactions. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Used to gather user consent under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK.
  • Google Firebase: Used for real-time multiplayer networking and leaderboard storage.

3. User Consent & GDPR (EEA / UK Users)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, we use Google's certified Consent Management Platform (CMP). You can choose whether to consent to personalized advertising, manage your consent preferences, or decline non-essential data processing at any time via the in-game consent dialog.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
