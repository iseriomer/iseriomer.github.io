About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at Istanbul University-Cerrahpasa (graduating September 2025) with a deep passion for game development. My Advancedise lies in creating engaging gameplay experiences through innovative mechanics and robust systems architecture using Unity.

From procedurally generated roguelike FPS games to strategic mobile puzzles, I focus on building highly replayable experiences with deep player progression systems. My work on HELLRAZE earned the "Best Technical Implementation" award at the 2025 Game Development Project Days.

I'm proficient in C# and experienced with modern game development patterns including Manager, State, Observer, and Factory patterns. I regularly share development insights on my YouTube channel, showcasing iterative design processes and technical implementations.
